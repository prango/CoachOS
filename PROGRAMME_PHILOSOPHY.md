# CoachOS programme philosophy

CoachOS is designed for sustainable strength and hypertrophy, not maximising a single workout. The best routine is the smallest effective dose that can be performed consistently, progressed cleanly, and recovered from.

## Principles

### Training is a feedback system

A programme is a hypothesis. Completed sets, symptoms, performance, sleep, and adherence update it. A template never proves work was completed or tolerated.

### Safety has priority over optimisation

Pain, illness, instability, and altered movement override a planned session. Reduce or omit a painful movement; do not automatically swap in a novel exercise. Escalate persistent symptoms to a qualified clinician.

### Progress only from demonstrated capacity

Use rep ranges and RIR. A load increase requires complete same-day readiness, stable symptoms, clean target sets, and at least two RIR at the progression threshold. Add a rep or the smallest practical load, never both.

### Dose volume gradually

Train a muscle about twice weekly when schedule and recovery allow. About 10 challenging weekly sets is a flexible reference, not a mandate. Add no more than roughly two hard sets per muscle per week after actual adherence and recovery support it.

### Preserve the signal

Separate completed from planned sessions, logged from self-reported RIR, and measured from estimated body-composition values. Do not infer effort from a template or treat default timers as observed rest.

### Use wearables as context

Compare wearable metrics with the athlete's own recent baseline. One unusual value is not a diagnosis and does not alone cancel training. Check subjective readiness and local workload first.

### Keep decisions explainable

State data used, data missing, the safety gate, planned dose, and the next progression criterion.
